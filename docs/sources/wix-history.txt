Team History
2000 - 2001: The Arcadia High School Robotics Engineering team was started by Richard Fretz. Richard was an engineer at JPL who became the teacher of the Advanced Robotics Engineering class. Since then, we have competed in FIRST Robotics Challenge almost every year.
​
2006: First year competing in the JPL Invention Challenge
​
2012 - 2013: Bill Chapman took over the Advanced Robotics class as the teacher and Robert Steele from the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) became the team's mentor
​
August 2013: The new Robotics Design and Production Labs opened as a part of the Arcadia High School Renovations. Located in rooms E-103 and E-104, the classrooms are state of the art engineering labs that allow simultaneous design, construction, programming, and testing of the robots in one location.
​
2013 - 2014: Sean Lin, a mechanical engineer from Raytheon became mentor. Raytheon became a team sponsor and the new Robotics Lab opened.
​
2014 - 2015: The Advanced Robotics Engineering class transitioned to the PLTW Engineering Design and Developement class (EDD). This introduced two other engineering courses, being Princples of Engineering and Intro to Engineering that lead up to this team.
2023: Bill Chapman retires and David Trent is hired as the new teacher for the EDD class
2025: Last year of JPL Invention Challenge
2026: First year of Wonderworks Competition
September 2026: This website was created

JPL Bucket Brigade Contest
This year, the JPL Invention Challenge was to move 8 liters of water from a resevoir into a bucket 16 feet away as fast as possible while satisfying many rules, such as no pumps allowed or no more than 2 liters of water can be travelling upwards at a single time.
​
Arcadia High School brought three teams: being ___, ___, and Still Water. All three teams competed in regionals and ___ along with Still Water made it to the finals at JPL.
​
At JPL, Still Water was able to achieve 1st place, moving the 8 liters in just 6.45 seconds. Its design featured an Archimedes Screw.
​
Overall, we were extremely glad to be able to end off JPL Invention Challenge on a good note.
03
Competitions
Members in the EDD class compete in the FIRST Robotics Challenge (FRC), JPL Invention Challenge, and Wonderworks. We typically focus on FRC in the second semester, while in the first semester, we train our members by having them compete in the other competitions, before transitioning into FRC, where the entire team works together to create a full-scale competition robot.
04
Upcoming Years
In the coming years, Arcadia High School is also preparing to expand its engineering facilities, giving programs like ours the potential to serve more students and take on larger projects. We are focused on our ability to compete, train new members, and build our program, which will allow us to fully use those opportunities in the future.
05
Fundraising
As a high-cost student engineering program with no school funding, we depend heavily on sponsors, grants, and in-kind support to keep these opportunities accessible. We are always looking for and open to sponsors to continue supporting engineering in Arcadia.
06
Outeach
Students in EDD participate in outreach events throughout the year to support STEM in any way possible. We believe that STEM should be accessible and taught to everyone. By teaching hundreds of students and helping others across the globe, we strive towards our mission of contributing to STEM every day.
