Interested in being a sponsor?
As a high-cost student engineering program with no school funding, we depend heavily on sponsors, grants, and in-kind support to keep contributing to STEM in our community. A robot takes more than just engineering. It starts with hundreds of individual components and students willing to turn them into one. As the cost of competing continues to rise, our priority turns to ensuring that we can participate in both of our engineering seasons.
​
Where your support goes: Our largest, single expense is FRC registration. It costs $7000 alone and is required to compete. Aside from that, in order to build the robot, we also need at least $6000 for materials such as motors, encoders, aluminum, steel, bearings, gears, etc. Our first semester competing in JPL/Wonderworks also requires many tools and materials that cost around $2000 for our three teams total.
​
As you can see, due to these high costs, we are required to heavily rely on sponsorships in order to continue our engineering team here at Arcadia. We have attached our full sponsorship packet below and thank you for your time.
